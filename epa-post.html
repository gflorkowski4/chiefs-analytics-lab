What is EPA, explained with Mahomes plays

2024 season · Kansas City Chiefs Data: nflfastR play-by-play

Yards lie. A 4-yard gain on 3rd-and-3 moves the chains; a 4-yard gain on 1st-and-10 barely moves anything. EPA — Expected Points Added — fixes that by asking one question: how many expected points did this play add?

The formula

EPA = expected points after the play − expected points before the play

What EPA doesn't do

EPA is descriptive, not destiny. It tells you what a play was worth, not why it worked or whether it will work again. That's what the Lab's explorer will let you check, play by play.
