WHAT IS EPA?
EXPLAINED WITH MAHOMES PLAYS.

Yards lie. A 4-yard gain on 3rd-and-3 moves the chains; a 4-yard gain on 1st-and-10 tells a different story. EPA asks a better question: how many expected points did the play add?

2024 SEASON KANSAS CITY CHIEFS DATA: NFLFASTR PLAY-BY-PLAY
01 / THE CONCEPT

A BETTER WAY TO VALUE A PLAY.

Expected Points Added (EPA) measures how much a play changes the points a team is expected to score from its game situation. Down, distance, and field position all matter — not just yards gained.

THE FORMULA

EPA = Expected points after the play − Expected points before the play

Positive EPA increases expected point value. Negative EPA decreases it.

03 / THE BIG PICTURE

THE SEASON IN ONE NUMBER.

Across the full 2024 regular season, Kansas City averaged +0.053 EPA/play on 1,392 plays with a 50.2% success rate. The source analysis reports Mahomes at +0.109 EPA/dropback across 617 passes.

Week 12 was the best (+0.244 EPA/play). Week 18 was the worst (−0.310) in a 38–0 loss with backups and only 49 plays. Week 6 was the bye: missing data is not zero data.

04 / WHAT TO REMEMBER

WHAT EPA DOESN'T DO.

EPA is descriptive, not destiny. It describes the value of a play in context — not the whole reason it worked, nor a guarantee that the same decision will work again.
